Sanctum runs your lights, sensors and automations on a small computer in
your home, such as a Raspberry Pi, and everyone in the household uses it from
the Mac app. It works without the internet, and your devices, automations and
data stay at home.

![The Sanctum Mac app in dark mode: the Home dashboard with each room's moods and lights](@meetsanctum/brand/readme-home.jpg)

## Where to start

[Installing Sanctum](/install/) takes a Raspberry Pi, or another Linux
machine, from nothing to a home with its first light in it. The pages after
it cover [updating](/install/updating/),
[what to do when something isn't right](/install/troubleshooting/) and
[removing Sanctum](/install/removing/).

[How Sanctum sees a device](/devices/) explains devices' parts and types,
the categories they show in, and what driver apps tell Sanctum about them.

[What apps do](/apps/) covers the apps in your home:
[adding and removing them](/apps/adding-and-removing/), what they're
[allowed to do](/apps/permissions/), and
[updates and problems](/apps/updates-and-problems/).

[Use Sanctum from an AI agent](/ai-agents/) covers adding an agent such as
Claude Code or Codex, and [what AI agents can use](/ai-agents/what-they-can-use/)
in your home.
