Everyone who lives in your home has a place in **Settings → People**: their
name and colour, which show who did what at home. Anyone can use the home's
shared devices, such as a wall display or a kitchen tablet, without signing
in. Someone who signs in can also use the Sanctum Mac app.

Every device that shows Sanctum sees the same People page and can do the
same things on it, a shared tablet too. Usernames show only on a person's
own page.

## Add a person

1. Open **Settings → People** and choose **Add a person**.
2. Type their name and choose a colour, then choose **Add**.
3. Sanctum asks whether they will sign in. Choose **Invite to sign in** to
   make their code now, or **Not now** for a child or a guest. You can
   invite them later from their page.

## Invite someone to sign in

An invite is a one-time code of 8 letters and digits, such as `K7QX-M4PD`.
The device you invite from makes the code, and Sanctum never sees it.

1. On the person's page in **Settings → People**, choose **Invite to sign
   in**.
2. Give them the code. Choose **Copy code** to send it in a message.
3. In the Sanctum Mac app, they choose your home, then
   **Just me** and **I have an invite code**.
4. They enter the code, then choose their own username and password. Their
   password never leaves their device.

The code works once, for 24 hours. You can't see it again after you close
it. To end it and make another, choose **New invite code** and confirm. To
end it without making another, choose **Cancel invite** and confirm.

After five wrong tries, a code stops working for 15 minutes, even if the
next try is right. Then it works again.

## Change your password

On a Mac signed in as you, open your own page: choose **Profile** in the
menu under your picture at the bottom of the sidebar, or your name in
**Settings → People**. Then choose **Change password** under **Sign-In**. Enter your current password,
then your new one twice, and choose **Change password**.

Your current password is checked on your Mac before anything changes, and
neither password leaves it. Your other devices stay signed in. To sign one
out, choose **Sign out** beside it under **Linked Devices**.

Change password shows only on your own page, on your own device. On someone
else's page, and on a shared device, it is **Reset password**.

## Reset a password

If someone forgets their password, open their page and choose **Reset
password**. Give them the new code: in the Mac app, they choose **Just
me**, then **I have an invite code**, enter it and choose a new password.
Their current password keeps working until the code is used, so a reset
started by mistake changes nothing. To end the code, choose **Cancel code**
and confirm.

## Remove a sign-in or a person

Both are at the foot of the person's page, each with a line on what it
does.

- **Remove sign-in** keeps the person in People. Their phones and
  computers sign out at once.
- **Remove _name_** removes the person, their sign-in, their devices and the
  AI agents they added.

Both ask first and say what ends. Your home always keeps one person who signs
in: the last sign-in can't be removed, and the page says so.

To sign out just one phone or computer, choose **Sign out** beside it under
**Linked Devices** on its person's page, or in **Settings → Linked
Devices**.
