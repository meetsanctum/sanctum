Most of what Sanctum does comes from apps. Moods sets the lights in a room,
Weather shows the forecast, and Zigbee brings in your Zigbee lights and
sensors. You add only the apps your home needs.

## The apps

| App           | What it does                                                          |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Moods         | Moods for each room, and what remotes and motion do with its lights   |
| Natural Light | Lights that follow the colour of daylight through the day             |
| Weather       | The forecast and sun times for where your home is                     |
| Zigbee        | Brings your Zigbee lights, plugs, remotes and sensors into your home  |
| ESPHome       | Brings your ESPHome lights, plugs, sensors and buttons into your home |
| Assistant     | Does what you ask a smart speaker, or type, for your lights           |

A new home starts with Moods, Natural Light and Weather. Zigbee and ESPHome
bring devices in, so Sanctum offers each of them once it sees something to
use it for, as
[Adding and removing apps](/apps/adding-and-removing/#zigbee-and-esphome)
explains.

## Finding your apps

Choose **Apps** from the tiles at the top of the sidebar. It lists your
installed apps, along with **Settings** and **Automations**. Press an app
to open it. An app with no page of its own, such as ESPHome, opens a page
in its colours that says what it does, with its version. **Show devices**
there opens **Settings → Devices**, where its devices are, and the gear at
the top opens its settings.

To keep an app close at hand, choose **Pin apps** at the top of Apps. Press
an app to pin or unpin it, then choose **Done**. Pinned apps sit at the top
of the sidebar, in the order you pinned them, on every device in your home.

**Settings → Apps** lists your apps with their versions. Press one to see
its settings: whether it's running, what it's allowed to do and what it adds
to your home. **Add Apps**, below it, lists the apps you could add.

## Each app runs on its own

Every app runs apart from the others, with its own share of memory. If one
stops working, Sanctum tries to restart it while the other apps carry on.
Sanctum controls which permissions an app has and shows them in the app's
settings.

What an app may do is up to you.
[Permissions](/apps/permissions/) explains what apps ask for and how to
change your answer.
