# Trademarks

Sanctum is a trademark, registered as a word mark in Switzerland. The
Sanctum logo, the knot with or without the name, is a trademark too.

Sanctum's code is open source, but its name and logo are not: the code's
licences, the AGPL and the Apache License, grant no rights to them
([README](README.md#licences)). The logo's files, the app icons and the
brand's photos are under a licence of their own,
[assets/brand/LICENSE](assets/brand/LICENSE).

This policy lets everyone talk about Sanctum and build on its code, while
people can still tell what is Sanctum and what is not.

## What you can do without asking

- Refer to Sanctum by name, accurately: in articles, reviews, videos, talks,
  courses, docs and posts.
- Say what works with Sanctum: "Lights for Sanctum", "works with Sanctum",
  "an app for Sanctum".
- Run Sanctum at home, changed or not.
- Share unmodified official builds of Sanctum under its name.
- Say that a fork is "based on Sanctum" or "a fork of Sanctum" in its
  description, as long as its own name and logo are clearly different.
- Use the logo, unmodified, to link to Sanctum
  ([assets/brand](assets/brand/README.md), Usage).
- Use the name for a user group or meetup that does not charge, such as
  "Sanctum users Zürich", as long as it is clear that Sanctum does not run
  it.

## What needs written permission

- Using the name Sanctum, or a name easily confused with it (Sanctum Pro,
  MySanctum, Sanktum), for a fork, a changed build, a product, an app, a
  company, a service, a domain or an account.
- Sharing a changed version of Sanctum under the name Sanctum, or with its
  logo or app icons.
- Selling hardware with Sanctum on it, or Sanctum as a hosted service, under
  the name Sanctum.
- Merchandise with the name or the logo.
- Suggesting that Sanctum made, endorses or certifies an app, device or
  service when it did not: "official", "certified", "approved".
- Using the knot, or something close to it, as a logo or an icon.
- Changing the logo, or combining it with another name or logo.

## Apps for Sanctum

An app's own name comes first: "Lights for Sanctum", not "Sanctum Lights".
Its icon is its own, not the knot and not one of Sanctum's app icons. Only
apps that Sanctum publishes are official.
